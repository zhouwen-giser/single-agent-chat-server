# Provisional WSGS geospatial consumer schemas

These WorldFinding and SourceProduct schemas come from the SACS task-package
proposal. They support S15-S17 fixture and consumer-development tests only.

They are not an authoritative WSGS handoff, do not establish a production
transport mode, and cannot satisfy the S14 or S24 live markers. Production
intake must replace or regenerate them from the WSGS-owned consumer lock while
preserving strict, failure-closed parsing.
